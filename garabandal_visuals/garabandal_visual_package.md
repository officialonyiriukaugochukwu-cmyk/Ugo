# THE REAL REASON GARABANDAL WAS NEVER APPROVED — Visual Production Package

**Style profile applied to every prompt:** Photorealistic documentary / archival live-action; muted desaturated documentary grade (cool grays, faded blacks, warm off-white paper) with sparse mustard-yellow or muted-red accents only on physical objects; available/practical light; locked-off or very slow observational camera; documents and objects dominate; high photorealism with 16mm grain; analytical, restrained, quietly ominous.

**Production note:** The angel and the Virgin are never depicted. Apparition moments are shown only through the girls' faces, light on skin, and empty sky or ground. Real, named individuals are not depicted by likeness: the bishop, the friar, the girls, and the commission are generic period figures.
